    Datasources

    Browse a remote collection and import a manuscript into the library — UTokyo and BDRC pages arrive as images, ready to OCR and translate; DharmaNexus texts arrive as text pages, ready to translate.

    Paste a CBETA citation — volume, text number, page, column, line — and the desk finds the passage in the Chinese corpus, with a CBETA link and one-click import.

    Settings

    Models come from your Ollama — nothing is downloaded by this desk. Pick an installed multilingual model.

    No Gemini key saved.

    The key is stored in the desk's local database and sent only to Google's Gemini API. Gemini is a cloud service: page text leaves this machine, unlike Ollama.
